# PHASE 6 - STOCK DETAIL & EXPLAINABILITY EXPERIENCE

---

## Objective

Plan how one symbol will be explained in depth.

---

## Detail View Sections

* symbol header
* final decision summary
* score and signal breakdown
* trend or recent price chart
* recent news list
* corporate action timeline
* full AI reason text

---

## Explainability Requirements

* show why the score is high or low
* show which inputs most influenced the decision
* separate numeric signals from AI narrative
* show when data is missing or stale

---

## Interaction Options

* open from results row or card
* back to results without losing context
* shareable URL for the symbol view

---

## Phase 6 Checklist

* [ ] detail sections defined
* [ ] explainability goals documented
* [ ] navigation back path defined
* [ ] missing-data handling documented
* [ ] chart and timeline areas planned
